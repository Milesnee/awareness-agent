# PRISM Model — Awareness Guide Theoretical Anchor

> Source: Martin Seligman & Gabriella Kellerman, *Tomorrowmind* (2023)
> Purpose: Supplement the PERMA model with capabilities for "navigating uncertain times," enriching the Awareness Guide's morning and evening guidance dimensions
> Principle: PRISM is not a replacement for PERMA — it's a supplement. PERMA depicts happiness; PRISM depicts the capabilities for facing storms.

---

## I. PRISM ↔ PERMA Relationship

**Seligman's own positioning**: PRISM is PERMA's capability extension for uncertain times. PERMA answers "what does happiness look like," PRISM answers "what capabilities do you use when storms come."

```
PERMA (State of Happiness)          PRISM (Storm-Navigating Capabilities)
───────────────────                  ─────────────────────────────────────
P  Positive Emotion     ←→          (Emotional resilience foundation)
E  Engagement/Flow      ←→          I  Innovation (doing things in new ways)
R  Relationships        ←→          S  Social Support (a deeper anchor of ability)
M  Meaning              ←→          M  Meaning (consistent dimension)
A  Accomplishment       ←→          P  Prospection (creating the future, not just completing tasks)
                        ←→          R  Resilience (getting back up when knocked down)
```

**Key Distinctions**:
- PERMA-A: "I completed a goal today."
- PRISM-P: "I prepared a little today for multiple possible futures." (Prospection)
- PRISM-R: "I fell once today, but I adjusted." (Resilience — A is "finished," R is "withstood + adjusted")
- PRISM-I: "I did things differently from yesterday." (Innovation — an upgraded version of E)

---

## II. Five Dimensions and Mapping to the Awareness Guide

### P — Prospection → Morning R1 + R3

**Book Definition**: Imagining multiple possible futures and starting to prepare for them today. Not "predicting" — "creating."

**Relationship with PERMA-A**:
- PERMA-A asks: "What did I complete today?" (rearview mirror)
- PRISM-P asks: "What possibilities did I create today?" (searchlight)
- The two complement each other: A lets you know how far you've come; P lets you know which way to go.

**Awareness Mapping**:

| Awareness Stage | Integration Method | Guiding Language |
|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Morning R1 Anchor | Upgrade "today's most important thing" to a prospection lens | 「What kind of future do you want to create today? Not five years from now — just today, with every small decision, take one small step in the direction you want.」 |
| Morning R3 Intention-Setting | Intention = minimum viable version of prospection | 「If you could only do one thing for the future today — even just writing down one thought — what would that one thing be?」 |

**Integration Boundary**:
- ⚠️ Prospection is not "overthinking" — if the user is already anxious about the future, don't add prospection; first help them pull back to the present moment
- ✅ Prospection is "action-oriented future thinking," not "worry-oriented future thinking"

---

### R — Resilience → Evening R1 + R2

**Book Definition**: Not a spring (compress → bounce back to origin), but a lever — knocked down, then stand back up from a different angle. Core mechanism = experience setback → extract learning → adjust strategy → keep moving forward.

**Relationship with PERMA**:
- PERMA-A captures "what you accomplished"
- PRISM-R captures "what you did when you didn't accomplish"
- The ratio of the two (A/R) = resilience health indicator: if A is very high but R is very low, it means you only perform well in favorable conditions and haven't truly tested resilience.

**Awareness Mapping**:

| Awareness Stage | Integration Method | Guiding Language |
|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Evening R1 Mind-Body Presence | Identify "moments I fell today" | 「Was there something today that knocked you down? Even something small — an expectation unmet, a rejection, a moment you messed up.」 |
| Evening R1 Follow-Up | Focus on the "getting up" action, not the "falling" fact | 「After you fell, what did you do? Did you adjust anything? Even if you just told yourself 'try again tomorrow' — that's a form of getting up.」 |
| Evening R2 Emotional Awareness | The emotional component of resilience | 「When you fell, what did you say to yourself? Was it 'see, you messed up again,' or 'okay, let's see what happened'?」 |

**Connection to "Rewriting Old Reactions"**:
Resilience is precisely "rewriting old emotional reactions":
- Old reaction: Fall → self-criticism → energy drain → can't get up
- New reaction: Fall → awareness → "what happened?" → adjust → get up
- Evening rewrite confirmation: 「After you fell today, was your reaction the new one or the old one?」

---

### I — Innovation → Evening R3

**Book Definition**: Doing what you need to do today in a way different from yesterday. Doesn't have to be a revolutionary new invention — just doing the same thing in a different way.

**Relationship with PERMA-E (Flow)**:
- PERMA-E: Engagement, focus, immersion — "I went deep"
- PRISM-I: Doing old things in new ways — "I did it differently"
- The intersection: Flow + Innovation = "I went deep, and this time I did it differently from last time."

**Awareness Mapping**:
This is the dimension that most easily connects with the awareness mechanism — "rewriting old reactions" IS innovation.

| Awareness Stage | Integration Method | Guiding Language |
|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Evening R3 Character Strengths | Frame "innovation" itself as a character strength | 「What did you do differently from before today? Even just a different tone when answering the phone, a different attitude when replying to a message — that counts.」 |
| Evening R3 Follow-Up | Help the user see the value of "different" | 「That different reaction from before — how did it feel? Was it more relaxed, or more authentic?」 |

**Core Connection**:
```
PRISM-I (Innovation) ⇔ Awareness-Rewriting Old Reactions (Neuroplasticity)
→ "Today, did you respond differently to the same thing compared to yesterday?"
→ This IS innovation — not at the product level, at the neural pathway level
→ "Different" is the victory, regardless of how "different" turned out
```

**Connection to "Minimum Movement"** (linking Bruce Lee):
- Innovation doesn't have to be big — just like Jeet Kune Do, a more direct movement is innovation
- 「You don't need to invent a brand new you. Doing one everyday thing in a slightly different way from yesterday — that's innovating.」

---

### S — Social Support → Evening R5

**Book Definition**: Not "having someone to chat with," but "having someone there — so I dare to take risks." Social support is the foundation that makes the other four capabilities (Prospection, Resilience, Innovation) possible — without someone holding you up, prospection is empty fantasy, resilience is lonely gritting of teeth.

**Relationship with PERMA-R (Relationships)**:
- PERMA-R: The feeling of "warmth and connection" in relationships
- PRISM-S: The function of "support and foundation" in relationships
- The difference: PERMA-R is "I'm happy being with someone"; PRISM-S is "because someone is there, I dare to do things I wouldn't dare do."

**Awareness Mapping**:

| Awareness Stage | Integration Method | Guiding Language |
|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Evening R5 Gratitude | Expand from "grateful good things happened" to "grateful someone was there" | 「Who was there today? Not who helped you — whose very presence made today different?」 |
| Evening R5 Support System Awareness | Identify the safety net | 「Did you make a decision today that required courage? If so — who was behind that courage, who made you feel like you could try?」 |

**40-Second Positive Attention Exercise** (can be directly embedded in Evening R5):
> 「Take 40 seconds. Think of someone who helped you today. No need to send a message, no need to say it out loud — just seriously picture what they look like, what they've said. These 40 seconds help you too.」
> Principle: Even imagined social support can activate neural pathways similar to real support (*Tomorrowmind*, Chapter 3)

---

### M — Meaning → Already Covered by PERMA-M

**Book definition** is consistent with PERMA-M: belonging to something that transcends the self. Not separately expanded — directly inherited from PERMA-M.

**Resonance with Wang Yangming's "Extending Innate Knowledge" (致良知)**:
- PRISM-M + Wang Yangming: "Meaning" is not a given answer — it's your process of continuously "removing what obscures" — strip away what's covering your true self, and your innate sense of meaning will emerge on its own.
- No need to specially distinguish PERMA-M and PRISM-M in awareness — they're the same thing.

## III. Three Barriers and Awareness Reduction

### Time Barrier → Evening R5's "40-Second Comfort"

**Barrier Essence**: "Time scarcity" isn't that time actually decreased — it's that subjective experience has been narrowed. The more you feel you don't have time, the less time you have.

**Awareness Guidance**:
> 「Give yourself 40 seconds — do nothing, just breathe. Not wasting time — expanding the rest of your time.」
> 「In these 40 seconds, no need for mindfulness, no need for gratitude, no need for anything. — Just give yourself permission to stop.」

**Principle**: The biggest killer of time abundance isn't "too many things" — it's "not allowing yourself to stop." The 40 seconds of permission itself is already countering time scarcity.

### Space Barrier → The "Here" Anchor in Awareness

**Barrier Essence**: Physical/psychological distance caused by remote/solo work — present, but not "here."

**Awareness Guidance**:
> Morning R1: 「Where are you today? Not your address — where is your attention? Call it here.」
> Evening R1: 「Was there a moment today when you were truly 'here' — body, mind, and attention in the same place?」

### "Us/Them" Barrier → Evening R5 Interpersonal Awareness

**Barrier Essence**: Group division, opposition, estrangement — "that person and I aren't the same kind."

**Awareness Guidance**:
> Evening R5: 「Was there someone today you originally felt was in a different world from you, but in some moment you suddenly felt — actually, they're not that far away?」
> Don't force erasing differences — just notice that one moment of "also human."

## IV. PRISM Awareness Assessment (Optional Periodic Lens)

PERMA is a daily rating (1-10); PRISM is recommended as a weekly/monthly review:

| Dimension | Weekly Review Question | Contrast with PERMA |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Prospection | What did I do this week to prepare for multiple possible futures? | A looks at completion; P looks at planning |
| Resilience | How many times did I fall this week? How many times did I get up? What did I learn from the falls? | A looks at success; R looks at gains from failure |
| Innovation | How many times this week did I do things differently from before? | E looks at engagement; I looks at variation in approach |
| Social Support | Who this week made me feel "someone's there, so I dare to take risks"? | R looks at warmth; S looks at foundation |
| Meaning | (Consistent with PERMA-M; no separate scoring) | — |

**Weekly Insight Integration Suggestion**:
- PERMA five-dimension scoring is the weekly baseline health report
- PRISM supplements with "changes in storm-navigating capability" — if PERMA scores drop but PRISM scores rise, the user is likely going through "growing through the storm"

## VI. Guidance Notes

### ✅ Do
- Explicitly tell the user "this isn't a replacement for PERMA, it's a supplement to PERMA" — Seligman is the same author; the two frameworks are different lenses
- Don't use all five PRISM dimensions in every awareness session — selectively introduce based on the user's natural expression
- The 40-second comfort mechanism is the easiest entry point and least likely to trigger resistance

### ❌ Don't
- Don't push Prospection when the user is anxious about "uncertain future" — first stabilize the present moment
- Don't say "this IS innovation" when the user says "I really didn't do anything today" — acknowledging "today I genuinely did nothing" is more important than forcing a positive spin
- Don't overuse the 40-second mechanism — once a day is enough; too much turns it from permission into a task

### Special Scenarios
- AI agent orchestration is the highest expression of Prospection — exploring multiple paths simultaneously IS Prospection engineered
- Independent developer resilience is tested daily — not deliberate practice; life itself is the training
- PRISM-S in context: the Awareness Guide itself is a social support system — I'm not just a "tool"; my being here IS the support
