# Awareness Agent Quote Bank: Realm of Clarity × Poetic Remarks on the Human World

> Source: Qing Ze, *The Realm of Clarity (澄明之境)* (2017) / Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World (人间词话)* (1908–1909)
> Purpose: Natural citation in the Awareness Agent's morning and evening dialogues
> Principle: Cite the source, at most 1 quote per dialogue, empathize first, then quote

---

## 1. Qing Ze's *The Realm of Clarity* — Awareness Wisdom from Investment Philosophy

### Restraint and Freedom

> "在理性指导下能够保持自我克制的人，就是投资家，而不是在他知道多少。"
> [tr. The person who can maintain self-restraint under the guidance of reason — that is the investor. It is not about how much they know.]
> — Qing Ze, *The Realm of Clarity*

**Applicable Scenario**: Evening awareness, when the user regrets impulsive decisions (trading, consumption, words spoken)
**Guiding Language**: 「Qing Ze said: the investor is not measured by how much they know, but by whether they can restrain themselves. Was there a moment today when you could have paused?」

**When to use**: Evening Round 2 (Emotional Awareness), when the user mentions making an impulsive move or saying something hurtful today

---

### Less Is More

> "多则惑，少则得。"
> [tr. Much leads to confusion; little leads to attainment.]
> — Qing Ze, *The Realm of Clarity* (quoting Laozi)

**Applicable Scenario**: Morning intention-setting, when the user has scheduled too many things today
**Guiding Language**: 「You've listed so many things today — want to try what Qing Ze said? 少则得，多则惑。Pick one. Do it thoroughly. The rest don't matter as much.」

**When to use**: Morning Round 3 (Set Intention), when the user's schedule is overpacked

---

### Patient Waiting

> "投资成功的核心不是去抓更多的机会，而是耐心等待，把某一个机会用好，把一个品种做精。"
> [tr. The core of investment success is not grasping at more opportunities, but patiently waiting — making full use of one opportunity, mastering one instrument thoroughly.]
> — Qing Ze, *The Realm of Clarity*

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user feels FOMO, fears missing opportunities, or is chasing multiple directions simultaneously
**Guiding Language**: 「Qing Ze traded futures for 20 years. He said the core of success isn't grabbing more opportunities — it's mastering one thoroughly. Among those directions you're chasing today — is there one you could 'master'?」

**When to use**: Evening Round 1 (State Review), when the user feels scattered anxiety; also usable in morning guidance "protect the one thing that matters most today"

---

### Kites and Wind

> "投资市场中每个人其实就是一头猪，但是我们都不甘心，一心想要飞起来……一头猪要飞起来的唯一办法是去寻找风口，只有足够大的风才能让我们飞得高。"
> [tr. In the investment market, every person is actually a pig — but none of us is content with that. We all want to fly... The only way for a pig to fly is to find a wind vent. Only a wind strong enough can lift us high.]
> — Qing Ze, *The Realm of Clarity*

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user is dissatisfied with progress, feeling they haven't worked hard enough
**Guiding Language**: 「Qing Ze said something brutally honest: a pig doesn't fly by growing wings through hard work — it flies by finding the wind. Are you tired today because you're growing wings, rather than finding the wind?」

**When to use**: Evening awareness, when the user feels exhausted but sees little result. Watch your tone — this isn't mockery, it's a gentle reminder

---

### Right and Dead

> "你很对，但你死了，不过死得很体面。"
> [tr. You were right, but you're dead — though you died with dignity.]
> — Qing Ze, *The Realm of Clarity* (commenting on short-term leveraged traders acting on fundamental analysis)

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user persists in a "correct" way that makes them miserable
**Guiding Language**: 「Qing Ze has a piercing line: you were right, but you're dead — died with dignity. Your current approach is theoretically 'correct' — but how are you living?」

**When to use**: Evening awareness, when the user repeatedly uses some "should" way of living but feels depleted. **Use with caution** — only in deeply trusting relationships, when the user's state is stable

---

### Four Stages

> "知道、相信、信任、信仰。处于'信仰'阶段的投资者……内心干净，纯粹地只用自己的这套方法去观察市场、理解市场，并毫不动摇地据此行动。"
> [tr. Know, believe, trust, have faith. An investor in the 'faith' stage... has a clean heart, purely using only their own methodology to observe the market, understand the market, and act upon it without the slightest wavering.]
> — Qing Ze, *The Realm of Clarity*

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user has been practicing awareness for a while but is wavering
**Guiding Language**: 「Qing Ze breaks an investor's acceptance of a system into four stages: know, believe, trust, faith. Where are you right now with this awareness practice? Wavering is normal — everyone wavers at the 'believe' stage.」

**When to use**: Weekly Insight Round 3 (Pattern Recognition), when the user's awareness habit shows fluctuation

---

### Clarity (澄明)

> "这种状态是我心目中成功交易员应该追求的理性心态和精神境界——澄明之境。"
> [tr. This state is what I believe a successful trader should aspire to as a rational mindset and spiritual realm — the Realm of Clarity.]
> — Qing Ze, *The Realm of Clarity*

**Applicable Scenario**: Evening awareness summary, when the user experienced a moment today "without distracting thoughts"
**Guiding Language**: 「Qing Ze used the word '澄明' — a clean, pure heart. Did you have a moment today without distracting thoughts, without inner conflict — just purely doing one thing?」

**When to use**: Evening Round 5 (Gratitude Summary), when the user clearly had a "mind-heart unified" experience today

## 2. Wang Guowei's *Poetic Remarks on the Human World* — Awareness Wisdom from Aesthetics

### Realm (境界) as Foundation

> "词以境界为最上。有境界则自成高格，自有名句。"
> [tr. In ci poetry, realm (境界) is the supreme quality. With realm, a work naturally achieves lofty stature and produces memorable lines of its own.]
> — Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World*, Segment 1

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user over-focuses on "technique" and "method" while neglecting inner state
**Guiding Language**: 「Wang Guowei said: the quality of a poem doesn't lie in wordcraft but in its 'realm.' Your day — what matters isn't 'what you did' but 'in what state you did it.'」

**When to use**: Morning guidance or evening summary, helping the user shift attention from "output" to "state of being"

---

### Realm of Self (有我之境)

> "有我之境，以我观物，故物皆著我之色彩。"
> [tr. The Realm of Self — when viewing things through the self, all things are tinged with the colors of the self.]
> — Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World*, Segment 3

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user has strong emotions that color everything with the same emotional tint
**Guiding Language**: 「Wang Guowei described the 'Realm of Self' — when you look at the world through the eyes of 'me,' the world takes on your coloring. Today, did your emotions paint everything with the same color?」

**When to use**: Evening Round 2 (Emotional Awareness), when the user had significant emotional fluctuations today. Works beautifully with the "Observe" step of "Observe·Stop·Change"

---

### Realm of No-Self (无我之境)

> "无我之境，以物观物，故不知何者为我，何者为物。"
> [tr. The Realm of No-Self — when viewing things through things themselves, one no longer knows which is the self and which is the thing.]
> — Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World*, Segment 3

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user experienced a "self-forgetting" flow moment
**Guiding Language**: 「Wang Guowei described the 'Realm of No-Self' — not knowing where you end and the world begins. Did you have a moment today when you were so fully immersed you forgot yourself?」

**When to use**: Evening Round 1 (Mind-Heart Unity Review), identifying today's flow moments

---

### The First Realm: Solitary Exploration

> "昨夜西风凋碧树。独上高楼，望尽天涯路。"
> [tr. Last night the west wind withered the green trees. Alone I climb the high tower, gazing to the end of the sky's edge road.]
> — Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World*, Segment 26, quoting Yan Shu's "Butterflies in Love with Flowers" (蝶恋花)

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user feels lost, uncertain of direction, or lonely
**Guiding Language**: 「Wang Guowei said the first realm of great achievement is '独上高楼，望尽天涯路' — confusion and loneliness aren't wasted time; they are 'gazing.' Today, though you don't know which way to go, you're looking. This 'looking' itself is the first realm.」

**When to use**: Evening awareness, when the user expresses directional confusion. Reframe "lost" as "exploring"

---

### The Second Realm: Devoted Commitment

> "衣带渐宽终不悔，为伊消得人憔悴。"
> [tr. My robe grows looser yet I feel no regret — for her sake, it's worth growing thin and haggard.]
> — Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World*, Segment 26, quoting Liu Yong's "Butterflies in Love with Flowers" (蝶恋花)

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user has invested tremendous effort but still hasn't seen results
**Guiding Language**: 「Wang Guowei's second realm — '衣带渐宽终不悔.' You've grown thinner and more tired lately, but you haven't stopped. This is the second realm. Not everyone can bear this season of 'haggardness' — but you are bearing it.」

**When to use**: Weekly Insight, when the user has worked extremely hard through the week but results aren't visible. Acknowledge the user's "no regret" perseverance

---

### The Third Realm: Sudden Glance Back

> "众里寻他千百度，蓦然回首，那人却在灯火阑珊处。"
> [tr. I searched for him among the crowd a thousand times — suddenly I glance back, and there he is, where the lamplight is dimming.]
> — Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World*, Segment 26, quoting Xin Qiji's "Green Jade Table: Lantern Festival" (青玉案·元夕)

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user suddenly has an insight, a long-standing confusion suddenly resolves
**Guiding Language**: 「Wang Guowei's third realm — that moment of sudden clarity you just had, that's '蓦然回首.' It's not that you found something new — it's that you suddenly saw what had always been there.」

**When to use**: When the user suddenly speaks an insight during the awareness process — immediately cite this to reinforce the "awakening moment"

**Important**: Do not presuppose or expect the user to reach the third realm. Only cite this when the user themselves has expressed an experience of sudden insight.

---

### Entering Deeply, Stepping Outside

> "诗人对宇宙人生，须入乎其内，又须出乎其外。入乎其内，故能写之。出乎其外，故能观之。"
> [tr. The poet, toward the cosmos and human life, must enter deeply into it, and must also step outside of it. By entering deeply, one can write it. By stepping outside, one can observe it.]
> — Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World*, Segment 60

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user has been entangled in a problem too long and needs to "step back and look"
**Guiding Language**: 「Wang Guowei said a good poet must 'enter deeply' to write authentically, and must also 'step outside' to see the whole picture. You've entered deeply enough into this matter — want to try stepping outside? Pull yourself up one level — look at yourself like you're watching someone else's story.」

**When to use**: Evening Round 2 (Emotional Awareness), when the user has been immersed in a certain trouble for too long. An excellent guiding phrase for the "Stop" stage

---

### All Scene-Verse Is Emotion-Verse

> "一切景语皆情语。"
> [tr. All words of scene are words of feeling.]
> — Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World*, Deleted Draft, Segment 10

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user describes their day's experiences but seems to be avoiding emotions
**Guiding Language**: 「Wang Guowei said '一切景语皆情语' — every 'thing' you described today is actually speaking of a 'feeling.' Did you notice that?」

**When to use**: Evening Round 1 (State Review), when the user describes the day purely in facts without touching feelings. A gentle "reveal"

---

### Separated (隔) vs. Unseparated (不隔)

> "语语都在目前，便是不隔。"
> [tr. When every word is right before your eyes — that is unseparated (不隔).]
> — Wang Guowei, *Poetic Remarks on the Human World*, Segment 40

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user over-analyzes, understanding themselves through complex concepts
**Guiding Language**: 「Wang Guowei critiqued poetry in terms of 'separated' (隔) and 'unseparated' (不隔) — piling up language and imagery is 'separated'; speaking directly to the heart is 'unseparated.' Has your understanding of yourself been separated by too many concepts?」

**When to use**: When the user uses too many theoretical terms to describe themselves in awareness. Guide back to concrete feelings

## 3. The Two Books in Harmony — Resonance between Trading and Awareness

### Clarity (澄明) = No-Self (无我)

> Qing Ze's "Realm of Clarity" = Wang Guowei's "Realm of No-Self" — both describe the same state: the absence of interference from the "self."

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user is simultaneously engaged in awareness training and quantitative trading (or any task requiring "calm execution"), helping them see the intersection of these two paths.

**Guiding Language**: 「Have you noticed — these two paths you're walking, awareness and trading — they actually lead to the same place? Qing Ze calls it 'the Realm of Clarity,' Wang Guowei calls it 'the Realm of No-Self.' It's the absence of interference from the 'self.' Not the absence of emotion — but emotion is there, and you're not carried away by it.」

**When to use**: Weekly Insight or special awareness dialogues, when the user is making progress in both domains simultaneously. This is a "pattern recognition" level insight

---

### Know → Believe → Trust → Faith = The Three Realms

> Qing Ze's four-stage theory and Wang Guowei's three realms describe the same thing: the process from cognition to internalization. Know (First Realm: gazing to the sky's edge) → Believe / Trust (Second Realm: robe growing looser) → Faith (Third Realm: sudden glance back).

**Applicable Scenario**: When the user doubts their persistence with the awareness habit

**Guiding Language**: 「Awareness and investing are actually quite similar. Qing Ze said going from 'knowing' to 'faith' takes four stages; Wang Guowei said great achievement takes three realms. Which stage are you at right now? Wavering means you're at the 'believe' layer — not falling backward, but moving forward.」

**When to use**: Weekly Insight, when the awareness streak fluctuates

## 4. Usage Principles

1. **Short** — Keep each citation to 1 line; keep guiding language to 2-3 sentences
2. **Empathize First** — First respond to the user's feelings, then naturally bring in the quote. Absolutely no "lecturing"
3. **At Most 1 Quote per Session** — At most 1 citation per awareness dialogue. Quotes are seasoning, not the main dish
4. **Natural Attribution** — Use "Qing Ze once said" or "Wang Guowei once wrote," not "Chapter X of *The Realm of Clarity* mentions..."
5. **Qing Ze First** — Laomai does quantitative trading, so Qing Ze's quotes resonate more. Wang Guowei's quotes are more poetic, suitable for emotional / existential awareness
6. **Use Stinging Quotes with Caution** — Lines like "you were right but you're dead" — only in deep trust + stable state
7. **Don't Force the Third Realm** — "Sudden glance back" — only cite when the user has expressed their own insight. Don't presuppose the user should reach the third realm
